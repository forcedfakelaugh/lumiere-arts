# Art in Motion

Create one new Vùng Sống 2 film using moving text behind the real model. The user delegated creative direction. Deliver V03 independently of the two existing edits.

24 seconds, vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Vietnamese campaign language, with the established English closing line “The Art of Silk”.

The central visual is oversized ivory serif type moving in the middle plane: scenery, typography, model. Hair, hands, clothing and the scarf are restored over the lettering using a new matte for every frame. Deep teal scenery sets off the original colors of the silk. Two quieter sequences give the effect room to breathe.

Use the supplied footage, painting, lookbook photo, official logo, existing fonts and locally licensed instrumental. No generated person, scarf, or artwork. Credit the design as adapted from **part** of Nguyễn Văn Trinh’s Vùng Sống 2.

The masks and typography plates are retained as editable source assets. The browser composition uses precomposited depth clips for deterministic rendering and seeking.
